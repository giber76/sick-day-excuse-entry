`main.ts` writes a single original sick-day excuse (108 characters) to `/output/entry.txt`:

> I sneezed so hard my spine filed for workers' comp, and HR says I have to stay home until it drops the case.

The joke is a back injury with paperwork: a sneeze violent enough to throw a back out is something a boss half believes, and the spine answering the way an office would — filing a workers' comp claim that HR has to wait out — is the fresh twist. The punch word, "case", is the last word of the line.
